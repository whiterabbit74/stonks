## SQLite schema

Copied from `server/src/db/index.js`: `dataset_meta`, `ohlc`, `splits`, `trades` (+ migrations), `calendar`, `sessions`, `broker_trades`, `telegram_watches`, `telegram_ema_alerts` (+ migrations), `webull_token`. WAL + foreign_keys. Driver: `modernc.org/sqlite` (CGO-free).

## Design tokens

- Fonts: Inter 400/500/600/700, JetBrains Mono 400/600 (`web/fonts/`)
- Tailwind utilities copied from current build CSS (`web/css/app.css`)
- Chart palette: `getChartColors` light `#ffffff/#1f2937/#eef2ff/#e5e7eb`, dark `#0b1220/#e5e7eb/#1f2937/#374151`
- Lightweight Charts v5 standalone UMD vendored (`web/vendor/lightweight-charts.standalone.production.js`): `LightweightCharts.createChart` + `chart.addSeries(LightweightCharts.CandlestickSeries|LineSeries|HistogramSeries)`
- Chart time: business day `{year,month,day}` from `YYYY-MM-DD` (no `Date`)
